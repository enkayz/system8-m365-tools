Before running it

Use your authorized customer workspace with Entra audit logs already routed to the AuditLogs table. You need workspace query access, such as Log Analytics Reader, and applicable Entra log-reading rights. Microsoft’s documented prerequisites include Entra ID P1 or P2. Customer ingestion, retention and query charges may apply.

Inspect the query, open your workspace’s Logs view, paste it and choose the time range before running. This page does not sign you in, request Graph permissions, configure a connector, grant roles or change a tenant. Do not enable data collection just to try this download without customer approval.

Microsoft: permissions and prerequisites for querying Entra logs

Read the output

The query matches Update service principal events with an AppAddress change, then groups redirect URI values by actor and service-principal name. It does not compare old and new values, prove effective access, or cover every app-registration change. App-only actors may have no user name.

Missing, denied, unrouted or out-of-range logs are not evidence of no changes. Verify your table schema and collection coverage before relying on the result.
